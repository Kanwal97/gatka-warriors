HOW TO PLAY

The nagara marks the beat. Strike on it for more damage, guard on it to PARRY, step on it to become untouchable. Heavier shastar beat slower — the drum you hear is your weapon's tempo.

Keep moving. Standing still weakens your guard and stops Balance recovery. Empty a foe's Balance to STAGGER them (red ring) for a heavy punish.

Chain 3 on-beat actions to enter FLOW (golden aura) — free combo-cancels and bonus damage.
