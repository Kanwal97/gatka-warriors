Production

Pipeline Notes

  • Meshes — blade / crossguard / grip / pommel / scabbard / gatra as separate elements; ~18–25k tris at LOD0, disc pommel and chapa on their own material IDs.
  • Damascus — tiling flow-mapped normal + roughness pair so the wave animates under the emissive, not baked-in painted highlights.
  • Engraving — on a trim sheet shared with the sheath furniture. Two emissive masks: fuller_flow (scrolling) and seal_burst (parry).
  • PBR — brass metallic 1.0, rough 0.35–0.6 graded by wear; velvet as anisotropic cloth, metallic 0, sheen on.
  • Rig hooks — tip-seal socket drives the slash-trail emitter; pommel-gem light intensity is scripted off the Simran / charge value.
  • Accessibility — parry-flare duration capped; a reduced-bloom setting honours photosensitivity and prefers-reduced-motion.
