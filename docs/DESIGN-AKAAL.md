# Character Bible — Akaal, "The Undying"

Canonical source for the character. The [dossier artifact](../akaal-dossier.html)
is the presentation of this; edit here first.

> **Pitch:** A Nihang Singh warrior-monk who fights with the stillness of a
> saint and the fury of a storm — every strike a prayer, every parry a vow.

## Identity

- **Name / title:** Akaal Singh, "The Undying" (*Akaal* = timeless, beyond death).
- **Background:** The last initiated guardian of a hidden *Bunga* (armoury-
  monastery) from the age of the Guru's warriors. Trained to protect the
  defenceless, not to conquer. Walks the world alone under a vow — no army, no
  throne.
- **Personality — the Sant-Sipahi (saint-soldier):**
  - Compassionate first — offers mercy before every fight, never strikes the fallen.
  - Fierce when drawn — *Nirbhau, Nirvair* (without fear, without hatred).
  - *Chardi Kala* — eternal high spirits; he fights **smiling**.
- **Battle cry / motto:** *Deg Tegh Fateh* (the kettle, the sword, and victory).
- **Setting flex:** grounded historical defender **or** neon "cyber-monk" (the
  Bunga as a temple-fortress) — same soul, different skin.

## Visual design — the Bana

Silhouette must read at a glance from character-select: **tall turban, flowing
robe, layered steel.**

- **Dumalla** (tall fortified turban) with a steel **Chakram** at the crown and a
  streaming saffron **Farla** pennant — his signature in motion.
- **Chola** (warrior robe) with **Kamarkasa** sash; short front / long back so it
  billows through Gatka spins without hiding leg reads.
- **Hajooria** — sacred cloth across the torso (teal), doubling as a combat wrap.
- **Dhal** (round shield, central boss) on the lead forearm; **Kara** (steel
  bracelet) anchoring the Iron-Shield ability.
- Palette: royal/Khalsa blue anchor + saffron accent + steel. See
  [ART-DIRECTION.md](ART-DIRECTION.md). For historically authentic construction
  see [COSTUME-BANA.md](COSTUME-BANA.md).

## Gameplay identity

- **Archetype:** Sant-Sipahi · **Weapon:** Khanda · **Resource:** Simran ·
  **Range:** Mid · **Difficulty:** read-heavy.
- **Combat style — "Flowing Steel":** mid-range momentum fighter. Weaves with
  Gatka footwork (dodges chain into attacks), punctuates with committed,
  super-armoured Khanda strikes. High skill ceiling: punishing to a masher,
  lethal to a patient player.
- **Abilities** (implemented — see [WEAPONS.md](WEAPONS.md) for data):
  1. **Chakram Storm** — fan of 3 discs across High/Mid/Low.
  2. **Sarbloh Kavach (Iron Shield)** — reflect the next hit from any vector.
  3. **Chardi Kala (Ultimate)** — invulnerable 360° Khanda spin, 2 AoE pulses;
     the "cannot die" fantasy made literal.

## Roster note

The enemy fighter is **"Vairi"** (crimson palette), who takes a different weapon
from the player each match for variety, and uses the same ability kit.
