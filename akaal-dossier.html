Identity

The Saint-Soldier

Akaal is the last initiated guardian of a hidden Bunga — an armoury-monastery that has stood since the age of the Guru’s warriors.

He trained not to conquer but to protect the defenceless — a living oath. When the sanctuaries were threatened he took up the full Bana and walked out into the world: no army behind him, no throne ahead, only the vow. He offers mercy before every fight and never strikes the fallen — yet once steel is drawn in defence, he is a storm, utterly without fear of death. This is the paradox the character is built to express: Nirbhau, Nirvair — without fear, without hatred.

He fights smiling. Victory and hardship are met with the same buoyant resolve — Chardi Kala, eternal high spirits — which colours his every battle-bark and drives the design of his ultimate.

Visual Design · The Bana

Attire & Silhouette

The silhouette must read from the character-select screen at a glance: tall turban, flowing robe, layered steel.

Dumalla

Fortified turban

A towering, layered dome — historically a mobile armoury. A steel Chakram is seated at the crown; a saffron Farla pennant streams from the peak as his signature in motion.

Chola

Warrior robe

A wrapped combat tunic cinched by the Kamarkasa battle-sash — cut short in front, long in back, so it billows through Gatka spins without hiding leg reads.

Hajooria

Sacred cloth

A length of cloth draped across the torso in a contrasting teal — practically doubling as a combat wrap that can be gripped, snapped, or used to bind.

Dhal & Kara

Shield & steel

A round Dhal with a central boss rides the lead forearm; the steel Kara bracelet anchors his Iron-Shield ability.

Playstyle

A mid-range momentum fighter. He weaves with spinning Gatka footwork — dodges chain into attacks — then punctuates combos with committed, super-armoured Khanda strikes.

High skill ceiling: punishing to a masher, lethal in patient hands. Rewards rhythm, spacing, and the read.
